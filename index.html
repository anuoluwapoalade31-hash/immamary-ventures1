LEADERSHIP & PURPOSE

Meet Our CEO

Alade Israel Founder & CEO

Israel is a graduate of Food Technology, a passionate leader, and an advocate for social impact and sustainable development. He is committed to creating meaningful change through community empowerment, environmental responsibility, and practical solutions to everyday challenges.

As the leader of IMMA MARY VENTURES, Israel is driven by a vision of expanding access to clean energy and reliable water for underserved communities and small-scale farmers. He believes sustainable development should improve quality of life while protecting the environment and creating opportunities for people to thrive.

Through innovation, service, and community-focused initiatives, Israel aims to help build a cleaner, healthier, and more resilient future for generations to come.

“Real progress means creating solutions that empower people, protect our environment, and strengthen communities.”
